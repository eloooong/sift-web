# Sift / first logo concepts

Status: five concept choices; no app identity has been replaced in this round.

## Brief

Sift helps Filipino adults assess suspicious messages, links, and screenshots.
The name is approved. The requested identity is minimal, calm, clear, and assured.
It must work in a compact website header and at small sizes, in black or reversed.
The existing slate palette and Geist UI remain the product context.
Assumption: the logo should be recognisable without relying on risk colours.

Category conventions to avoid: shield-and-check combinations, padlocks, blue
security badges, circuit brains, gradients, and fine scanner crosshairs.

Word map: sift / separate / retain / grain / clarity / passage / inspect / fishy.

## Exploration

Scores are design judgements out of five: clarity, distinction, simplicity,
relevance, and small-size strength, respectively.

| Idea | Construction | Scores | Decision |

| Through | A retained dot passes between two rows of filter bars. | 4 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 5 | Build B |

## Construction and intended tradeoffs

- A uses two mirrored curved bands and a 45-degree separation. Compact and bold;
  an S is a crowded category, so the seam and silhouette must carry distinction.
- B uses four rectangular bars and one circular retained grain. Closest link to
  the name; its meaning is abstract before someone learns the brand.
- C is a single fish silhouette with a generous eye cutout. Memorable and friendly;
  it is the most playful option and can suggest a fishing product without context.
- D is hand-drawn lettering with a shared f/t crossbar. Quietest and most name-led;
  the full word needs more width than an icon at favicon scale.
- E uses chamfered corners and a 30-degree diagonal spine. Strong in a small tile;
  the tone is more technical and angular than the others. Its first square-spined
  iteration resembled a 5, so the second iteration opens the diagonal.

All logo lettering is drawn as vector paths, without an external font or live SVG
text. Concept-sheet labels use the renderer's system font and are not logo art.
Library references were studied for construction, not traced: Pagekit, Auth0,
Kotlin, and Framer. The concept comparison is not a trademark-clearance search.
Trademark clearance should precede commercial registration.

Recommend A for the best balance of a clear initial, compact silhouette, and calm
authority. B is the strongest alternative for meaning tied directly to “Sift.”
