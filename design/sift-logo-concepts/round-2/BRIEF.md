# Sift / wider creative exploration

The user clarified that “minimal” applied to naming, and approved a wider range
of logo personalities. This round deliberately explores colour, character,
editorial typography, bold lettering, and an intricate emblem. It supersedes the
minimal-only assumption of round 1. All five choices remain concepts.

The product still helps Filipino users assess suspicious messages and links.
No new claim of guaranteed safety, government affiliation, or verification is
introduced. No product code, PRODUCT.md, or DESIGN.md is changed in this round.

## Concepts and tradeoffs

- **A / Current:** fluid, two-colour ribbons paired with wide Bricolage Grotesque.
  Most versatile in a digital identity; less literal connection to fraud detection.
- **B / Scout:** an original owl mascot with a reading lens and asymmetric details.
  Most memorable character; playful rather than clinical and needs a simplified
  small-size version if selected. Not traced from the ESDoc reference owl.
- **C / Fieldnotes:** Fraunces serif typography paired with a folded-page initial.
  Editorial and human; the folio can suggest publishing without the wordmark.
- **D / Offbeat:** staggered, rotated Fredoka lettering with a contrasting i.
  Most outspoken; less formal than a conventional security brand.
- **E / Watchful:** an engraved eye with concentric instrument ticks and small
  typography. Intentionally detailed; outer details need simplification for icons,
  and eye symbolism may feel watchful or imply surveillance to some viewers.

Recommendation: B has the most recognisable personality; C offers the best
editorial counterpoint. A is the broadest digital direction.

## Sources and construction

Original vector drawing and outlined type. Font binaries and the corresponding
SIL Open Font License texts are retained in fonts/. Sources are the google/fonts
repository on GitHub, ofl/fraunces, ofl/bricolagegrotesque, ofl/archivo, and
ofl/fredoka. The SIL OFL allows use of these fonts in logos. The font files retain
their original names; logo text is converted to paths with fontTools.

The first round already presented grayscale concepts. This second round uses
colour to show the wider styles requested, with separate reduction and monochrome
checks. Colour is an identity exploration rather than a change to the app's risk
colours or UI palette. No gradients or glass effects.

Studies: ESDoc and Giantswarm for mascot construction, Koa for editorial
letterforms, and the skill's category library for visual conventions. References
are not traced. Trademark clearance has not been performed.
